# People, Process, AI

**Making innovation deliver impact: AI for the charity sector**

👉 **[View the guide: superjammy.github.io/people-process-ai](https://superjammy.github.io/people-process-ai/)**

The guide explains, at a high level and with practical examples, how charities can go from experimenting with AI to real impact for the people they serve. It covers:

- The 10-20-70 rule: why people and process matter more than the technology
- The four levels of AI use, from chat interfaces to a fully connected AI harness
- Agent autonomy, data strategy and digital skills
- A 12-week AI adoption programme and a 30-day starter checklist
