Myths and facts

Clear up four common myths first

Most of the worry about AI comes from a few misunderstandings. Deal with them early so your team can get started.

MYTH“AI will replace our staff”
FACTSo far it saves about 3% of time, with no measurable effect on jobs yet.
MYTH“AI means less work”
FACTSaved time quickly fills up with new work.
MYTH“AI is always right”
FACTIt can be confidently wrong, so always check it.
MYTH“What I type stays private”
FACTOnly put personal data into approved tools.
The Jevons paradox: when something becomes more efficient, people use more of it, not less. Plan for this and protect your team's wellbeing.

Source: Humlum & Vestergaard (2025), Large Language Models, Small Labor Market Effects, NBER w33777.

Level 3 in practice

Agent autonomy: who's in the driving seat?

Example: a supporter emails to update their details. How much should AI do on its own?

1
ASSISTANT

You do it, AI helps

You ask, then make the change yourself.

2
COPILOT

AI suggests, you decide

AI drafts the reply, you check and send it.

3
AGENT

AI acts, you check

AI makes the change and flags anything unusual.

4
AUTONOMOUS

AI acts within rules

AI handles it, you review weekly.

← More human controlMore autonomy →
Rule of thumb: the more sensitive the task, the more human sign-off it needs.
